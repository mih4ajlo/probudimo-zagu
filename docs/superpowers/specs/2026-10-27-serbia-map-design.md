# Serbia map overworld — design

Date: 2026-10-27
Status: approved in conversation, awaiting written-spec review
Target release: v0.5.0 (branch `feat/serbia-map`)

## Intent

Replace the green test field with a zoomable, cartoon-style map of Serbia.
The princess (Zaga) walks across the country and wakes every public
prosecutor's office listed in `assets/tuzilastva.csv`, shouting
"Budite se, na posao!" at each one.

## Decisions (from the brainstorm)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Map role | Replaces the overworld. She walks on the map. |
| Map art | Drawn by the game from real borders (Natural Earth), cartoon style. |
| Borders | Serbia **including** Kosovo; Kosovo is walkable land (no offices there). |
| Stops | Every office in the CSV is its own stop (60). |
| Rendering approach | Vector shapes drawn at runtime (not a pre-rendered image, not tiles). |

## Gameplay

- Loop stays: title → wake scene → map → ending.
- She starts in **Beograd**.
- Walking into an office marker: shout pose + `SHOUT_LINE` as the first
  dialog line, then that office's reply. Dialog blocks movement, as now.
- Points only on the first visit of each office; repeat talks are allowed.
- Ending when all 60 offices have been visited.
- E-key shout, left/right facing, walk/shout animations: unchanged.

### Levels and points

The three lord ranks are removed. Office level sets points:

| Level | Meaning | Points | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OJT` | osnovno (basic) | 10 | 27 |
| `VJT` | više (higher) | 20 | 25 |
| `AJT` | apelaciono (appellate) | 30 | 4 |
| `PJT` | posebno (special) | 40 | 3 |
| `VRHOVNO` | Vrhovno javno tužilaštvo | 50 | 1 |

Max score: 27·10 + 25·20 + 4·30 + 3·40 + 50 = **1060**.

### HUD

`Score 120/1060  Offices 7/60` on the existing dark strip.

## Data (all in `src/data/`, no Kaplay imports)

- `offices.ts` — `Office { id, name, level, city, points }`, `OFFICES`
  (60 entries), `LEVEL_POINTS`, `maxScore()`. Generated once from the CSV
  and committed (the CSV stays in `assets/` as the source).
  - `id`: stable slug, e.g. `ojt-novi-sad`, `prvo-ojt-beograd`, `vrhovno`.
  - `name`: as in the CSV (trimmed, double spaces collapsed).
  - City names fixed for the CSV's encoding damage: `šabac` → `Šabac`,
    `Pošarevac` → `Požarevac`. Addresses, phones and e-mails are not used.
- `cities.ts` — city name → `{ lon, lat }` for every city in `OFFICES`
  (entered by hand; ~33 cities).
- `serbia-geo.ts` — generated by `scripts/make-map.py`: Serbia+Kosovo
  outline ring(s), named rivers (polylines), neighbour outlines with names;
  all in lon/lat, simplified, tens of KB.
- `replies.ts` — a few placeholder reply lines per level (copy can change
  without touching code).
- `lords.ts` / `lords.test.ts` are removed; `story.ts` stays.

## Map

### Source and generation

`scripts/make-map.py` (run once, output committed):
1. Download Natural Earth 10m admin-0 countries and rivers GeoJSON.
2. Take Serbia (`SRB`) and Kosovo; write both rings (inside = in either).
3. Neighbours within the bounding box (HUN, ROU, BGR, MKD, ALB, MNE, BIH,
   HRV) as context outlines.
4. Rivers: Dunav, Sava, Tisa, Drina, Velika/Južna/Zapadna Morava, clipped
   to the view box.
5. Douglas–Peucker simplification (hand-written; no shapely), rounded
   coordinates, written as TypeScript.

The game never downloads anything at runtime.

### Projection and scale

Equirectangular around Serbia's mid-latitude (x scaled by cos(lat₀)),
north up. Serbia is ~1600 game units tall (≈3.3 units/km), so a straight
north–south walk at the current speed (80 u/s) takes ~20 s.

### Look (cartoon)

- Serbia: warm flat fill, thick dark outline, soft offset shadow.
- Neighbours: muted fill, thin outline, name label.
- Rivers: blue polylines, wider downstream.
- Cities: dot + name in the pixel font.
- Offices: small building icon coloured by level (OJT grey, VJT blue,
  AJT green, PJT purple, VRHOVNO gold); a check mark once visited.
  Offices of one city sit on a small ring around the city dot.
- Font: also load Press Start 2P `latin-ext` so č ć š ž đ render.

### Zoom and camera

- Mouse wheel and `+`/`-` zoom in fixed steps.
- Range: from all of Serbia on screen (min) to today's close view (max).
- Camera follows her, clamped to the map bounds.
- City labels hide when far out; office names show only when close.

### Movement and collision

- Free movement; the move is accepted only if her feet stay inside the
  Serbia+Kosovo outline. If a diagonal move is blocked, try x-only, then
  y-only, so she slides along the border.
- Offices are touch triggers (no solid body). Wall tiles and the green
  field are removed.

## Testing (Vitest unless noted)

- **Offices:** 60 entries, unique ids; level counts 27/25/4/3/1; points
  match `LEVEL_POINTS`; `maxScore()` is 1060; every city has coordinates;
  every city lies inside the outline.
- **Geo:** Beograd, Niš, Subotica, Priština inside; Budapest, Sarajevo
  outside; projection keeps north up and is consistent; a blocked
  diagonal move slides along the border; a move fully outside is refused.
- **Layout:** offices of one city do not overlap and stay within a fixed
  radius of the city.
- **Zoom:** clamped to [min, max]; each step is a fixed factor.
- **Score/progress:** first visit grants the office's level points, repeat
  grants 0, unknown id grants 0; ending needs all 60.
- **Playwright smoke:** unchanged (page loads, canvas visible, no
  `pageerror`).
- Not tested: drawing, colours, label placement, camera smoothness.

## Out of scope

Indoor office scenes, branching dialog, travel/fast-travel, save/load,
real office photos or logos, a minimap, Cyrillic copy, addresses or
contact details in the game.

## Risks

- Hand-entered city coordinates: tested for being inside the outline;
  small offsets are acceptable for a cartoon map.
- Kaplay 3001 concave polygon drawing: if the outline does not fill
  correctly, draw it as triangles from a simple ear-clipping step in
  `src/geo.ts` (covered by tests).
- Frame rate with many shapes: offices and labels are culled when far out.
